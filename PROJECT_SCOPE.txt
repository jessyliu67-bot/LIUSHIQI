BIG BANG V35 is the existing local management application and offline resource exporter.
The clean seed database contains 300 stable store/template identities.
The canonical V35 archive contains 300 Shopify bundles and 300 SHOPLINE bundles.
Shopify bundles contain 4 Liquid files, 1 CSV, and 7 policy files.
SHOPLINE bundles contain 3 SLINE files, 1 migration CSV, and 7 policy files.
SHOPLINE product loading uses native all_products reads and keeps a configured fallback.
Offline resource checks do not prove live storefront rendering, product import, image rights, legal correctness, or platform approval.
The separately supplied original executable has no included native launcher source or reproducible build project.
